I met Preston – a friend from university – recently after many years, this old website came up during our conversation, and it was his birthday so I promised to dig it out from my archives.

Back in the uni days, he wanted to have a website to display his photos and blog on, and wanted to be cooler than everyone else just using Geocities, so we created this Flash site for him with animations, music, and a striking black and white theme to emphasise the content itself.

He kept updating it for several years after launch, but unfortunately that is lost with time, so it's only the launch content I could restore.

I did have to fork Ruffle and do a little fix to make images work in the HTML textfield so that blog posts would render, but otherwise it was relatively easy to get it running, I'm still amazed by this library and so happy that it's making it possible to experience more internet history.

In case you're wondering, it was done with Flash 7 / ActionScript 2, set to a 1008×714 stage to fit in 1024×768 screens of the time.

The "CMS" was a few `.txt` and `.xml` files that needed hand editing via FTP, this was before I learnt any backend development 😅

I had to remove the music from the jukebox as now it's hosted on my website and I don't want music industry lawyers on my back so you might want to put some tunes on yourself after the intro finished playing!
